Privacy Policy for Toolz

Effective Date: November 26, 2026
Developer: AP Labs
Contact: theaplabsofficial@gmail.com

1. On-Device Processing

Toolz (com.aplabs.toolz) is an offline-first utility app. All images, PDFs, audio, video, text, and calculations you process in Toolz stay 100% on your device. We do not collect, upload, or store your personal files on our servers.

2. Permissions Used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you actively use the Voice Recorder tool to record audio locally to your device.
  • Flashlight / Camera Flash (FLASHLIGHT): Used only when you toggle the Flashlight & SOS tool. Toolz does not capture or record camera photos or video.
  • Media & Files: Accessed only through Android's system Photo Picker and Document Picker when you explicitly choose a file to edit or convert.
  • Network & Internet: Used by Google AdMob to load banner advertisements.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Toolz displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Toolz is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact AP Labs at theaplabsofficial@gmail.com.
